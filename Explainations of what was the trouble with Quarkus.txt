Hello, for all that came to our live coding, this is the fix branch and the explanations of the concerns.


In fact, when I did my tests with Quarkus I let some code inside that was doing injections of services and chatmodule.

When we did the demo, I removed everything, but... In building phase Quarkus is removing all beans that are detected as not injected.
And the beans we used using programmatic lookup were not detected (even the chatModule producer).

To prevent that, we should annotate with @Unremovable, wich is not possible because we want to keep code compatbile with other CDI implementations, or the bean should be @Named or have an @Observer.

To do that proprely I rewrite the two extensions and invite you to check the code, using @Priority I can detect @RregisterAIService annotations and collect the "tools" used,
then I can ehance the "tools" with an @Named if necessary.

I also rewrite the AzureOpenAIChatModuleExtension to provide it in synthetic form.
